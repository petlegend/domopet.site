For veterinary professionals

Median Diagnostics rapid tests

The second line we work on alongside pet food. A rapid test gives the vet an answer during the appointment — no sample sent to a lab, no second visit for the owner.

This page is written for professionals. The tests are intended for veterinarians and clinics. Supply terms, the items available at any given time and the accompanying documents are agreed case by case — write to us.

The manufacturer

Median Diagnostics

A Korean company that has developed and manufactured veterinary diagnostic kits since 1999. Its plant is in Chuncheon, Republic of Korea.

The focus is infectious disease in animals. The range covers rapid tests (VDRG®), PCR kits (VDx®) and ELISA kits (VDPro®).

Manufacturing and certification

  • GMP for veterinary in vitro diagnostic devices (2024)
  • GMP for in vitro diagnostic devices (2021)
  • ISO 13485:2016 — quality management system (2020)
  • ISO 9001

As stated by the manufacturer. We send copies of the certificates on request.

Tests for cats and dogs

What the range covers

Below are the manufacturer's rapid tests for small companion animals. Ask us about a specific item before you plan around it: what we hold depends on the batch.

Kit Species What it detects
VDRG® CaFe Influenza Ag Cats, dogs Influenza virus antigen
VDRG® CIV Ag Dogs Canine influenza virus antigen
VDRG® E.canis Ab Dogs Antibodies to Ehrlichia canis
VDRG® Giardia Ag Cats, dogs Giardia antigen
VDRG® Leishmania Ab Dogs Antibodies to Leishmania
VDRG® SFTSV Ag Cats, dogs SFTS virus antigen

A rapid test result is something the vet decides on, not a diagnosis in itself. Only a veterinary professional can read it and prescribe treatment.
