Where to buy Pure Nature

Domopet is the distributor: we import the food and hold it in our own warehouse in Moscow. Retail orders are taken by the shops listed on this page — and the first of them is ours.

We recommend

Domokup — our own online shop

The full Pure Nature range, a gift with every order, coupons, and scheduled delivery — the next bag arrives on its own, about when the last one runs out.

Domokup is run by us, not by a reseller. The stock it ships comes from the same Moscow warehouse, so what the site shows as available really is on the shelf.

Close to home

At veterinary clinics and pet shops

Pure Nature food and Ever Dream litter are not online-only. We ship them to veterinary clinics and pet shops directly from the Moscow warehouse.

Ask for Pure Nature at your own clinic or shop. If they do not carry it, write to us — we will point you to the nearest place that does, or offer the shop supply terms.

Write to us About the company
