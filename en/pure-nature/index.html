The brand

Pure Nature, made in Korea

Pure Nature is a Korean line of dry food for cats and dogs. Domopet represents it in Russia: we import it officially and answer for the paperwork, the dates and the storage conditions of every batch.

One thing worth knowing: other makers sell food under the same Pure Nature name. What we supply is the Korean one, from Je-il Feed Co., Ltd..

Who makes it

Je-il Feed Co., Ltd.

The food is produced by Je-il Feed Co., Ltd., a plant of Harim, which has been in the feed business since 1960. Production sits in South Korea and the food travels to us from there with no repacking on the way.

How quality is held
01

HACCP and ISO 9001

The plant runs a HACCP system recognised by the Korean Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs: raw material and finished food are checked for microbiological safety. The plant also works to ISO 9001.

02

Formulated to AAFCO and FEDIAF

Recipes are built to meet the AAFCO and FEDIAF nutrient profiles. Those are industry nutrition standards for what a complete diet should contain — not a certificate awarded to a particular batch.

03

Human-grade raw material

The maker's own rule is "100% human-grade quality, 0% synthetic preservatives": ingredients fit for human food, with no synthetic preservatives or flavourings added.

What is in it

In the recipe, and left out of it

The base is fresh meat and fish, with vegetables, fruit, probiotics and vitamins added. There are no by-products, no cereals, no preservatives, colourings or flavourings.

Hydrolysed protein

The protein is hydrolysed — the molecules are broken into smaller fragments, which the body recognises more easily. That makes the food suitable for animals with food sensitivity.

Grain-free

No wheat, no maize, no other cereals. Carbohydrates come from other ingredients instead — sweet potato in the dog recipes, for example.

A pack inside the pack

The contents of every bag are split into sealed sachets:

Pack Sachets inside
2 kg 200 g × 10
5 kg 500 g × 10

While one sachet is being fed, the rest stay closed. That beats leaving the whole bag open for weeks — which is what happens in a one-animal household, where a pack lasts a long time.

Clearance for Russia

Approved by Rosselkhoznadzor

Pure Nature is made by Je-il Feed Co., Ltd. — as of today the only Korean pet food plant approved by Rosselkhoznadzor for supply to Russia.

Domopet is that plant's exclusive distributor in Russia. A declaration of conformity and veterinary documents exist for every batch, and we send them on request.

The register is public and can be checked again at any time. It can also change, which is why the claim is stated as of today.

Scans of the declaration of conformity and the veterinary paperwork are being prepared for publication. Until they are up, ask us by email and we will reply with them.

Composition, calorie content and feeding tables are published only from official manufacturer data. They are available on request.
