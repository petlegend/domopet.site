Grain-free, hydrolysed protein

Cat litter

Ever Dream clumping litter

Bentonite litter for the cat tray. We have carried it as long as we have carried the food, and it is in stock now.

Bentonite is a natural clay. On contact with moisture it gathers into a clump that is scooped out, leaving the rest of the litter usable. The whole tray therefore needs changing less often than with absorbent litters.

Keep a layer of roughly 5–7 cm so a clump can form all the way through, scoop daily and top up to the old level. Clumps go in the household waste, not down the drain.
