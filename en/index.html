Why Pure Nature
01

The only Korean plant cleared for Russia

Pure Nature is made by Je-il Feed Co., Ltd. — as of today the only Korean pet food plant approved by Rosselkhoznadzor for supply to Russia. Domopet is its exclusive distributor here.

The plant belongs to Harim and has been in the feed business since 1960.

02

A pack inside the pack

The large bag is split into 200 g and 500 g sachets. Feeding is simple, and the food does not sit open for weeks.

03

Super-premium, functional

A complete diet with functional ingredients — protein, fats, vitamins and minerals in the balance an animal needs every day. The protein is hydrolysed, so the food suits animals with food sensitivity.
